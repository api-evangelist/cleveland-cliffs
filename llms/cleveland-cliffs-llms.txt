# Cleveland-Cliffs

> Cleveland-Cliffs Inc. (NYSE: CLF) is North America's largest flat-rolled steel producer and the
> largest supplier of steel to the automotive industry, operating an integrated business from iron
> ore mining and pelletizing through steelmaking, finishing, tooling and stamping. Cleveland-Cliffs
> publishes no developer portal, no public API and no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract. Its
> documented machine-to-machine surface is batch EDI - ANSI ASC X12 version 4010 over SFTP or an
> IBM Sterling VAN - with 23 implementation guidelines published publicly as PDFs. Its transactional
> portals (Outside Processors, iSupplier, Customer Service Center, Vendor Inquiry) are login-gated.
> This file was generated by API Evangelist from a third-party profile; Cleveland-Cliffs does not
> serve an llms.txt of its own (probed 2026-09-05, HTTP 404).

## Integration surface

- [EDI specifications for outside processors](https://www.clevelandcliffs.com/doing-business/for-outside-processors): ANSI ASC X12 v4010 for Burns Harbor, Cleveland, Indiana Harbor and Kote. SFTP preferred, VAN via IBM Sterling Supply Chain Business Network Hub. 18 inbound and 5 outbound transaction guidelines plus a testing template.
- [Supplier information](https://www.clevelandcliffs.com/doing-business/for-suppliers): iSupplier portal, vendor inquiry, invoice submission routes, supplier quality manual and purchase order terms.
- [Customer information](https://www.clevelandcliffs.com/doing-business/for-customers): price book, monthly surcharge tables, claims policies and terms and conditions of sale.
- [Product compliance and certification](https://www.clevelandcliffs.com/doing-business/product-compliance): IATF 16949, ISO 9001, ISO/IEC 17025 (A2LA), PED and ABS certificates as dated PDFs.
